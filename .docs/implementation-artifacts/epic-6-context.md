# Epic 6 Context: The group plays the Board from one shared screen

<!-- Generated from planning artifacts. Regenerate with compile-epic-context if planning docs change. -->

## Goal

The Host names two to four Teams and runs the round from a TV or projector mirrored from the laptop. The Host makes scored Reveals, marks misses, undoes misclicks, passes turns, reveals the rest without points, moves through the Questions and ends on the final Scores. Every move is saved on the server, so the round survives a reload. Play is the payoff of the whole loop. When this epic is done, Stage 1 is complete end to end, proved by a no-key, no-network end-to-end test and an automated accessibility pass. Play must never show anything except the locked Board: no raw Answers, no discarded Groups and no Groups below the Board limit.

## Stories

- Story 6.1: Name the Teams and start play
- Story 6.2: Show the Board on the shared screen and make scored Reveals
- Story 6.3: Misses, undo, turns and unscored Reveals
- Story 6.4: Next Question, final Scores and the played Game
- Story 6.5: A full round without a model key, end to end

## Requirements & Constraints

- **Teams:** play starts only from *ready*, with two to four Teams named 1 to 14 characters. Every Score starts at zero and the Game moves to *playing*. One Team, five Teams, an empty name or a name over 14 characters is refused, and a Game in any other state gets `WRONG_STATE`.
- **Board display:** one Question at a time. Each Board slot is a hidden tile that shows only its rank, so the number of rows shows how many Groups there are. The screen also shows the current Team and every Score. On a 1920×1080 window, Question, labels, Counts, Team names and Scores render at 48 px or more. A 200-character Question with eight Groups must still fit 1080 px with the control bar visible.
- **Scoring:** a scored Reveal shows the label and Count and credits the current Team. A Score always equals the sum of the Counts of that Team's scored Reveals. Revealing an already revealed slot is refused (`ALREADY_REVEALED`), and so is a slot on another Question (`WRONG_QUESTION`). An unscored Reveal changes no Score.
- **Miss:** a miss is visual only. It changes no saved state, does not pass the turn and cannot be undone.
- **Undo:** undo applies only to the most recent Reveal on the current Question, scored or unscored. It hides the Group again and subtracts the Count from the Team that received it. Undo with nothing to undo is refused, and earlier Questions' Reveals can never be undone.
- **Turns and moving on:** the next Team is chosen by hand and wraps from the last Team to the first. The next Question is chosen by hand, and its unrevealed Groups stay hidden. After the last Question the final Scores show, the Game moves to *played*, and the Host is told the deletion date (30 days after play ends). Every play operation on a *played* Game is refused with `WRONG_STATE`.
- **Persistence:** the current Question, current Team, Reveals and Scores survive a reload.
- **No key (FR-30):** `tests/e2e/no-key.spec.ts` runs with no key and every non-localhost request blocked. It goes through register, create, Questions, links, three Answer sets from three browser contexts, close, fallback Grouping (Review shows the `NO_KEY` Notice), one merge, mark all reviewed, lock, two Teams, scored and unscored Reveals, one undo, and play to "Sluttstilling". Each final Score must equal that Team's scored Counts. An axe pass over the answer form, Review and the play view finds no serious or critical violations. No Token or Answer text from the spec appears in the server output.
- **Quality bars:** NFR-5 (each play action shows its result within one second), NFR-6 (WCAG 2.1 AA), NFR-4 (play view at 1920×1080), all UI in bokmål. The manual items A1, A7, A8, A9 and the play timing check are recorded in `tests/manual/checklist.md`. Every state-dependent operation has a `WRONG_STATE` case and a foreign-Host `NOT_FOUND` case. FR IDs go in test titles and rows in `tests/TRACE.md`.

## Technical Decisions

- **Module (AD-1):** a new `src/server/play` module owns `team`, `play_state` and `reveal`, and exports no writers. It may call earlier modules only. It reads the Board only through `review.getBoard`, which Story 6.2 adds to `review` (the owner of `board_slot`; the story cites AD-15). Play never touches `answer`, `answer_group` or `grouping_result`.
- **Tables (AD-16):** `team(id, game_id, name 1..14, position)`, `play_state(game_id PK, current_question_id, current_team_id)` and `reveal(id, game_id, board_slot_id unique, team_id nullable, seq)`. A null `team_id` means an unscored Reveal. Foreign keys use `ON DELETE CASCADE` toward `game`, and the migration is generated with `npm run db:generate` and committed. The Team name limit of 14 belongs in `src/lib/limits.ts`.
- **Operations (AD-16):** reveal takes only `{ boardSlotId, scored }`. The Team comes from `play_state` under the Game lock, never from the client. Undo deletes the highest-`seq` Reveal on the current Question. The next Question is the one with the smallest greater `question.position`, since positions are dense and frozen after *draft*. When there is no next Question, `finish` transitions to *played* (`games.transition` already sets `played_at`) and returns `deleteAfter = played_at + 30 days`.
- **Score (AD-11):** Score is never stored. It is the SQL sum of `board_slot.count` over a Team's scored Reveals. `board_slot.count` was frozen when the Board was locked.
- **State and authorisation (AD-4, AD-5):** every mutation starts with `games.lockForMutation` for the states in `src/lib/game-state.ts`, which already defines `startPlay: ready`, `playMove: playing` and `finishPlay: playing`. `ready → playing` and `playing → played` go only through `games.transition`. `boardSlotId` and `teamId` are resolved inside the owned `gameId`, and any miss is `NOT_FOUND`.
- **Actions and errors (AD-17):** mutations are Server Actions in `src/app/spill/[id]/actions.ts` through its `hostAction` helper (Story 6.1 put `startPlay` there), with the zod schemas in `src/lib/play.ts`. They return `{ ok: true, data } | { ok: false, code, message, fieldErrors? }`, are re-validated in the module entry point, and call `revalidatePath`. There is no client data library, no websockets and no new Route Handler. New codes (`WRONG_QUESTION`, `ALREADY_REVEALED`, nothing-to-undo) get bokmål messages in `src/lib/errors.ts`. The play view renders from saved server state.
- **Tests (AD-22):** integration tests run on fresh `pglite://:memory:` with the real migrations. A unit property-style test covers arbitrary Reveal and undo sequences (Score equals the sum of scored Counts). E2E runs on `next build && next start` with replay. There is no component-test library, so UI behaviour is proved by e2e and axe.
- **Retention (AD-23):** the existing purge already deletes Games 30 days after `played_at`. Play only has to set it and show the date.

## UX & Interaction Patterns

- **Routes:** `/spill/[id]/skjerm`. The *ready* Game page shows "Brettet er låst. N spørsmål er klare." and "Start spillet". A *playing* Game offers "Fortsett spillet". A *played* Game page shows the final Scores, "Vis sluttstilling" and the info notice "Spillet og alle svarene slettes automatisk {dato}.". Other states redirect to the Game page.
- **Team setup (light Host page):** heading "Hvem spiller?", fields prefilled with "Lag 1" and "Lag 2", "Legg til lag" up to four, "Fjern" down to two. Errors read "Laget trenger et navn." and "Navnet kan ha maks 14 tegn.". "Start spillet" comes with the hint "Trykk F for fullskjerm.".
- **Canvas:** a fixed 1920×1080 layout scaled with `transform: scale()` and letterboxed. Stage colors apply, and there is no Host top bar. The main column is 1312 px and the Team column 448 px, with a 32 px gap and a 64 px gutter. Vertical budget: 48 px top, 221 px Question block (56 px up to 80 characters, 48 px above, four lines), 24 px gap, 696 px list, 24 px gap, 64 px control bar. Row height is `clamp(80px, (696px − 8px × (n − 1)) / n, 104px)`. A revealed label never truncates: it wraps to two lines at line-height 1.05 and the row grows to 112 px.
- **Tiles and Teams:** a hidden tile is a `<button>` named "Avslør plass N". While a click waits for the server, its border is `stage-ink`. A revealed tile is cream with a forest Count badge and is no longer a button. An unscored tile is dashed, with "uten poeng" in its accessible name. No berry anywhere on the stage. "Spørsmål N av M" sits at the top of the Team column. The active Team is a mint card with "Svarer nå" at 48 px. Team names are 48 px and Scores 80 px with tabular figures.
- **Control bar (24 px, `stage-muted`):** "Bom [M]", "Angre [Z]" (hidden when there is nothing to undo), "Neste lag [T]", "Vis resten uten poeng [U]" (a toggle with `aria-pressed` that reads "Avslører uten poeng [U]" in `stage-ink` weight 700, dashes the hidden tiles, and switches off on the next Question), "Neste spørsmål [N]" (or "Vis sluttstilling" on the last Question), and "?". Shortcuts: `1`–`8`, `M`, `Z`, `T`, `U`, `N`/`→`, `F`, `?`, `Esc`. They are ignored while focus is in a text field.
- **Motion (CSS only, no library, no sound):** the staged Reveal takes about 700 ms and starts after the server acknowledges. Tile fill takes 180 ms. The label fades in with a 12 px slide from 120 ms. The Count badge scales 0.9 → 1 from 300 ms. The Score counts up over 400 ms from 460 ms in `stage-accent`. "Bom" is an 80 px panel on `stage-raised` with a 2 px `stage-line` border: 150 ms in, 1.2 s hold, 150 ms out, with no red and no icon. Under reduced motion everything is instant and the colour change is kept. There are no tile flips and nothing imitates the TV show.
- **Live region:** "«{label}», N poeng til {Team}." and "Angret. «{label}» er skjult igjen, N poeng trukket fra {Team}.".
- **States:** while loading, the stage shows "Henter spillet …" in `tv-team`. When every tile is revealed, the hint "Alle svarene er avslørt." appears and "Neste spørsmål" is emphasised. A save failure shows the banner "Fikk ikke lagret trekket. Prøv igjen." in place of the 64 px control-bar row, and a lost connection shows "Mistet forbindelsen." in the same banner. The banner is never over the list and never red, and the state stays unchanged.
- **Final Scores:** "Sluttstilling" shows the Teams sorted by Score and centred vertically, with Scores at 120 px. The winner gets the active card look with "Vinner", or every tied Team gets "Uavgjort". Below them come the 24 px line "Spillet slettes automatisk {dato}." and "Tilbake til spillet".
- **Accessibility and voice:** the focus ring on the stage is `focus-inverse`, targets are at least 44 px, state is never shown by colour alone, and the copy uses bokmål "du" with no exclamation marks. The right end of the control bar is reserved for the Stage 2 source credit. UI work uses `impeccable` together with `ponytail`.

## Cross-Story Dependencies

- 6.1 comes first. It creates the `play` module, `team`, `play_state` and their migration, Team setup and the play view shell. 6.2 adds `reveal` and `review.getBoard`, and builds the Board, tiles, Score and reload on top of it. 6.3 adds undo, turns and unscored Reveals to the 6.2 control bar. 6.4 needs `play_state` and the Question order. 6.5 needs all of them.
- Builds on epic 5: `board_slot` (position, label, frozen Count, nullable `group_id`), the *ready* state and "Brettet er låst. N spørsmål er klare." on the Game page.
- Builds on epics 1 to 4 for 6.5: the server-output capture (Story 3.3), the fallback Grouping with the `NO_KEY` Notice, and the existing e2e fixtures and network guards.
- Feeds epic 7: Demo mode and the hand-in checks reuse this play path, the manual checklist items and the no-key spec. Stage 2 (strikes and steals, assisted Guess matching) extends `play` inside AD-16 later.
