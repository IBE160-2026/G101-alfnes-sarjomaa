- (event by user) Idea chosen: a Family Feud-style party game where the host builds their own question form. Self-defined project, not one of the eight proposals from the course staff - so staff feedback on the idea should still be requested.
- (event by user) Origin material: a real housewarming party used a Google Form ("Leilighet til leilighet") with 15 questions about named people in the friend group, anonymous free-text answers, then a manually tallied Family Feud round off the results.
- (decision by user) Product name is Toppsvar - ASCII, no spaces, so the BMAD run folder and shell paths stay clean. G23 shipped "brief-COSI - MRP-2026-09-13" with spaces and had to URL-encode it everywhere.
- (decision by user) Primary user is the HOST who creates and runs the game; participants are secondary - because all the manual labour (writing the form, chasing answers, tallying free text, building the board, keeping score) sits with the host, while the participant experience today is already fine.
- (decision by user) Free-text answers are clustered by AI into groups, and the host can override (merge, split, rename) before play. This is the project Beslutningspunkt that the course requires every project to name, and it keeps a human in control of the one judgement call the game depends on.
- (decision by user) Brief is written entirely in English, headings and body - matching the instructor exemplar product-brief-beergame.md. Note this differs from reference group G23, which used English headings with a Norwegian body. The rest of the repo documentation stays Norwegian.
- (decision by claude) Collection is asynchronous and play is live and co-located: the form is filled in the days before, the game is played together off one shared screen. Derived directly from how the origin party actually ran.
- (decision by claude) No participant accounts in the first version; the host shares a link or code. Anonymity is treated as a product requirement rather than a privacy footnote, because the source form promised anonymity while Google displayed the respondent account - the answers depend on that promise being credible.
- (decision by claude) Brief is placed at .docs/planning-artifacts/briefs/brief-Toppsvar-2026-09-14/brief.md (the BMAD convention, matching both example repos and the instructor beergame repo), with a byte-identical copy at the repo root as product-brief.md because the assignment names that filename. The run-folder copy is the only one ever edited.
